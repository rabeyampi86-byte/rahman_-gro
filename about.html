About Rahman Agro
& Poultry Farm

Rahman Agro & Poultry Farm is a growing poultry farm dedicated to raising healthy and quality Broiler and Sonali Chickens. Our Farm manages approximately 3,000 to 4,000 birds and focuses on proper care, feeding, hygiene, and a healthy environment for better growth. We carefully manage our poultry birds throughout their growing period and follow proper farming practices to maintain their health and quality.

Our Farming Practices

Proper management is an important part of successful poultry farming. At Rahman Agro & Poultry Farm, we focus on providing appropriate care to our birds during their growing period. Feeding is an essential part of this process, and maintaining a suitable feeding routine helps support healthy development. We also pay attention to cleanliness and hygiene because maintaining a clean environment is an important part of responsible poultry farming.

We regularly monitor the condition of our birds and try to maintain suitable conditions within the farm. Good care is not limited to feeding alone; it also involves maintaining the farm environment and paying attention to the birds throughout their growth. Through consistent management and careful attention, we work toward maintaining healthy and quality poultry birds.

Our Poultry Birds

Rahman Agro & Poultry Farm mainly raises two types of chickens: Broiler and Sonali. Both types are managed at our farm throughout their growing period before they are supplied to local retailers. Our focus is to raise the birds with proper care and provide them with suitable conditions for healthy growth.

Broiler chickens are an important part of our poultry operation, while Sonali chickens are also raised according to the needs of the local market. By working with both types of poultry birds, we are able to serve different requirements of local retailers and contribute to the regular supply of poultry birds in our area.

Our Supply & Retail Network

After the birds reach the appropriate stage for supply, Rahman Agro & Poultry Farm provides them to local shopkeepers and retailers. Our relationship with local retailers is an important part of our farm and connection with the customers in the local market. We aim to maintain a reliable supply of poultry birds so that our retail partners can continue to meet the needs of their customers.

We believe that a successful poultry business depends not only on raising healthy birds but also on maintaining good relationships with the people and businesses we work with. For this reason, we value reliability, consistent service, and responsible farm management as important parts of our business.
